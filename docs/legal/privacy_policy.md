# TimeSense Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

TimeSense is built by Born Royal LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what
you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version: TimeSense collects what it needs to tell you what to do next, and nothing else.
We do not sell your data, we do not advertise, and we do not track you across other apps or
websites.

---

## What TimeSense collects

**Your account.** Your email address and a user ID, through Firebase Authentication. If you sign in
with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever see Apple's relay address.

**The tasks you capture.** Their text, your estimates, deadlines, and when you completed them and
how long they took. This is the core of the product — it is what the recommendations are built from.

**Your calendar, if you connect it.** Event titles, times, and locations, so TimeSense can find the
gaps in your day and avoid recommending something into a meeting. TimeSense only writes an event to
your calendar after you approve that specific event.

**Health and activity, if you allow it.** Sleep and wake times, steps, active energy, exercise
minutes, and workouts, read from Apple Health. These are used to estimate your energy through the
day, which is why a demanding task is suggested when you can actually do it. **TimeSense never
writes to Apple Health.**

**Locations you save, if you allow it.** When you save a place like Home or Work, its name and
coordinates are stored so errands can be recommended when you are near where they can be done. If
you grant Always access, TimeSense is notified when you arrive at or leave one of those saved
places. **TimeSense does not record a continuous location history or a trail of where you have
been.**

**Voice captures, if you use them.** Speech is transcribed to text — on-device where iOS supports
it — and only the text is sent to us. **Raw audio is never uploaded and never stored.** Storing raw
audio would require your explicit, separate opt-in, which the app does not currently offer.

**Basic diagnostics.** Crash reports through TestFlight and the App Store, and server logs that
record which requests were made and when. These are used to fix faults.

## What TimeSense does not do

- We do not sell or rent your personal data. Ever.
- We do not show you ads or share data with advertising networks.
- We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app declares no tracking
  domains.
- We do not read the contents of your files or documents. The app has no file upload.

## Who else sees your data

We use a small number of service providers, each of which processes data on our behalf:

| Provider | What it handles |
|---|---|
| Firebase Authentication (Google) | Sign-in, account identity |
| Render | The TimeSense API and its PostgreSQL database |
| OpenAI | Turning what you type or say into a structured task |
| Stripe | Payment details, for web subscriptions. We never see or store your card number. |
| Apple | App distribution, TestFlight crash reports, in-app purchases |

Text you capture may be sent to OpenAI to be interpreted into a task. It is not used to train their
models. We share your data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

## Where it lives and how long we keep it

Data is stored in the United States. We keep it while your account is active. Delete your account
and we delete your tasks, your saved places, your health and activity records, and your calendar
mirror. Backups roll off within 30 days. Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you
may be kept.

## Your choices

**Every permission is optional and separate.** Calendar, Health, Location, Microphone and
Notifications each have their own iOS prompt, and TimeSense works without any of them — it simply
knows less and recommends less well. You can change any of them at any time in iOS Settings →
TimeSense, and the app will stop collecting that category immediately.

**Your rights.** You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete
it. Depending on where you live (the EEA, the UK, California, and elsewhere) you may also have the
right to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection
authority. Write to the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

TimeSense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you
believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes
effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
